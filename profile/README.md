## Africa as builder, not buyer.

Next-Gen Collective is a youth-driven movement in South Africa, mobilising young Africans through entrepreneurship, AI and innovation, policy and wealth creation.

**[Next-Gen Factory](https://github.com/next-generation-collective/factory)** is how we put the movement to work. It checks the work you build with AI, proves it works, and learns from every builder who uses it. Free and open source.

```bash
npx @next-generation-collective/factory
```
